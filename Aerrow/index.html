Aerrow

Aerrow is personal passion project with the goal of making engineering concepts more intuitive and engaging. I did not come from a strong fundamental fondation in math as I was not a good student in high school, I finished my 4 years with a GPA below 3.0 and my last math grade being a D. After 5 years away from structured instruction and having not done any math beyond basic algebra I came back and received an A in Calculus I, II, III, and Differential Equations. Learning engineering is not easy, I want Aerrow to be a great resource for all aspiring academics and become a bridge for non-traditional students, a place that makes complex topics feel intuitive and easy, similar to Paul's Online Math Notes or the Organic Chemistry Tutor. Although I began learning fundamental engineering concepts at 24, I want to make engineering available to people from 10 to 100 years old.

It is important to know that anyone can engineer, but only the fearless can be great.
